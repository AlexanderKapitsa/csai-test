longAbout: >-
  The Academic Council of the Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence
  Institute is a collegial body that considers key issues of educational,
  scientific, and organizational activity of the faculty. The council consists
  of the dean, deputies, heads of departments, lecturers, and student
  representatives. Meetings are held regularly according to the work plan.
