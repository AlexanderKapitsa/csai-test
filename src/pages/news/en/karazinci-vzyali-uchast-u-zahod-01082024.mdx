On 27 November, Acting Director of ESI ‘FTF’ Pylyp Kuznetsov and Head of the Department of Electronics and Control Systems of the Faculty of Computer Science Maksym Khruslov made a presentation on the advanced methods of conducting laboratory work at Karazin University in the context of distance learning within the framework of the event "4EU+ Coffee Talk: Unconventional Digital Education" organised within the framework of the 4EU+ for Ukraine project.

The meeting was open to anyone interested in digital education: researchers, teachers, students, doctoral students and the general public. The event also created an opportunity to meet people working in other European universities, as well as to share and discuss various experiences related to Chat GPT, virtual reality, the use of digital tools in teaching, etc.
