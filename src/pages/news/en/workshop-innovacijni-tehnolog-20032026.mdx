On March 18, 2026, a Workshop “Innovative technologies of coding and information protection for non-rooted platforms” was held for masters and teachers of the Institute of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence of the V. N. Karazin KhNU in order to popularize the scientific direction of the department “Development of methods and technological solutions for steganocompression coding and information protection using artificial intelligence systems”. 

The organizer and speaker of the event was the head of the scientific direction, Doctor of Technical Sciences, Professor, Professor of the Department of Mathematical Modeling and Data Analysis of the Institute of Computer Science and AI Volodymyr Viktorovych Barannik.
