On 20 October, we hospitably opened our doors to future students and their parents. Visitors had a unique opportunity to immerse themselves in the technological atmosphere of the institute, learn about our educational programmes related to computer science and artificial intelligence, and choose their path to a successful future in IT.

The event was held offline, in a safe environment, and gathered more than 600 students who were interested in the opportunities of our university.

Representatives of the institute presented key areas of study, shared the features of programmes that train highly qualified IT specialists, and spoke about the prospects for the development of artificial intelligence.
