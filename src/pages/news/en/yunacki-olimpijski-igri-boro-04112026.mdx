Khazar Heydarov, a first-year student at the Educational and Scientific Institute of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, has brought Ukraine its first medal at the 4th Summer Youth Olympic Games — and it is gold! He became the champion in the under 66 kg weight category, claiming the first award for the Ukrainian national team at these Games!

17-year-old Khazar began his tournament run in the quarterfinals, where he confidently defeated Matar Diop, representing Senegal. In the semifinals, he overcame Hungarian judoka Nimrod Mildner, securing his spot in the final.

In the decisive gold-medal bout, Khazar faced the Cadet World Champion, Anes Mati from Canada. As the regulation time ended with no scores recorded, the fate of the Olympic gold was decided in overtime — the "Golden Score" period. Four minutes into this extra time, the Ukrainian’s opponent received a third penalty (shido), handing the victory — and the top prize — to our judoka!

Congratulations to Khazar Heydarov and his coaches on this brilliant achievement!
