We congratulate Yurii Pohuliaiev, a lecturer at the Department of Mathematical Modeling and Data Analysis, on the successful defense of his dissertation in the field of Software Engineering (Specialty 121). His thesis is titled "Models and Methods of Software Development for Biometric Data Classification Based on Minutiae Parameters."

The defense took place on August 27, 2026, at a meeting of the Specialized Academic Council (DF 64.052.057) at the Kharkiv National University of Radio Electronics.

We wish him inspiration and continued professional success!
