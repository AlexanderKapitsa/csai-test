On 15 September 2026, a joint meeting of the working groups for the development of educational-professional and educational-scientific programs of the Institute of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence was held, with the participation of the Institute's management, heads of educational programs, and members of the working groups of educational programs, during which the results of the internal audit and preparations for the accreditation of the Institute's educational programs were discussed. During the discussion, the shortcomings identified during the internal audit and current issues related to the preparation of the Institute's staff for the upcoming accreditation were discussed. A roadmap of the nearest urgent measures was developed.
