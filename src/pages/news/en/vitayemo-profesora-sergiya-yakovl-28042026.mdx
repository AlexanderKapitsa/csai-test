We sincerely congratulate Professor of the Department of Mathematical Modeling and Data Analysis, Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, Corresponding Member of the National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine, Serhii Yakovlev, on being awarded the state prize — the S.O. Lebedev Prize (for outstanding achievements in the field of computer engineering, instrument engineering, and the development of automation and control systems) — for the series of works “Models and Methods of Computational Intelligence for Swarm Mission Planning and Area Coverage with Ensured Security of Distributed Systems”.

We wish you inspiration and continued success in your scientific endeavors!
