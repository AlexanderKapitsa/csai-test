We are pleased to announce that our project **"The Way to IT with Karazin University"** is once again opening its doors to young enthusiasts in the world of information technology! **From February to May**, we successfully held regular career guidance games for schoolchildren and lectures given by professionals to introduce them to various areas in the IT sector.

Our events have become a great opportunity for young people to learn more about the prospects and opportunities in the IT sector, discover new horizons and get inspired by experienced professionals. The meetings were held **every Saturday**, and we are pleased to announce that these events will continue in **September**!

All events were held in a mixed format, which allowed us to organise safe attendance for all participants.

We invite everyone to join us in September and continue their journey into the world of IT together with Karazin University. Follow our news and do not miss the opportunity to become a part of this exciting journey!

**Details of the upcoming events and the schedule can be found on our social media channels.**
