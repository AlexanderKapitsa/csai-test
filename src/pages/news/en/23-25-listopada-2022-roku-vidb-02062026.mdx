On November 23–25, 2022, the 8th International Scientific and Technical Conference "Computer Modeling in High-Tech Technologies" (CMHTT-2022) took place at V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University. The event brought together researchers, academics, PhD students, and university students from Ukraine and international institutions to discuss current issues in mathematical modeling, information technologies, cybersecurity, and software development. As a result of the conference, a collection of scientific papers was published, containing the outcomes of contemporary research in the fields of computer modeling and high-tech technologies.

More information about the conference proceedings is available at: [CMHTT-2022 Conference Proceedings](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1zRMqc5jDDCXG2xFf42e4n8LzfOAxy7kc/view?usp=drive_link).
