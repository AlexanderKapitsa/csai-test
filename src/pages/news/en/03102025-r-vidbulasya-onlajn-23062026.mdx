On 3 October 2025, an online meeting took place between representatives of the Educational and Scientific Institute of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (ESI CSAI) of V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University and students of Kharkiv Gymnasium № 148.

The students were addressed by the Heads of Departments: Associate Professor V. M. Strukov, Associate Professor O. I. Oleshko, Associate Professor M. M. Khruslov, Associate Professor M. V. Yesina, and Associate Professor S. V. Diadiun. They provided information about studying at the Institute, the academic specialities offered, the main areas of activity of the Institute’s departments, and employment opportunities after graduation. They also answered the students’ questions.

The Principal of Gymnasium № 148, O. V. Liubukhina, invited representatives of the Educational and Scientific Institute of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence to hold an offline meeting in the safe educational space of the Kharkiv Metro (Peremoha station) in a broader format with the participation of students from other lyceums.
