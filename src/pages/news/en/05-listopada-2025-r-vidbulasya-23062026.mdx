On 5 November 2025, an online meeting took place between representatives of the Educational and Scientific Institute of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (ESI CSAI) of V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University and graduating students of Kharkiv Physics and Mathematics Lyceum № 27.

The graduates were addressed by the Director of the Educational and Scientific Institute of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Associate Professor D. Yu. Uzlov, the Heads of Departments, Associate Professor V. M. Strukov, Associate Professor O. I. Oleshko, Associate Professor M. M. Khruslov, Associate Professor M. V. Yesina, and faculty members Associate Professor S. V. Diadiun and Associate Professor O. Ye. Sporov. They introduced the students to their departments, provided information about admission to and study at the Institute, the academic specialities and educational programmes offered, employment opportunities after graduation, as well as opportunities for international internships and parallel study simultaneously at the ESI CSAI and partner universities in Europe. The speakers also answered the students’ questions.
