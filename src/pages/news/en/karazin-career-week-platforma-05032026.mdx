Karazin Career Week is a key event of the year dedicated to the development of career prospects and the professional realization of young talents. Our event unites the academic community and IT market leaders to create a successful future.

### What is this event about?

We have created a space where theory meets practice, and students meet their future employers:

● **Career Orientation:** Live meetings with representatives of leading partner companies of our institute.

● **Deep Dive:** Detailed information about current professions in the fields of Computer Science and AI.

● **Expert Dialogue:** Q\&A sessions with specialists who are already changing the digital industry today.

● **Career Start:** The opportunity to be the first to learn about current job and internship offers.

● **Networking:** Effective communication and networking between business and students.

### Invitation for stakeholders (employers)

The National Research Institute of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence is actively forming a presentation base for partners within the framework of Karazin Career Week. We invite your company to join the event and tell about yourself to our talented youth.

Do you want to post a vacancy or present your company to our students? Please fill out the form for partners:

### What we offer to partners:

1\. **Digital presence:** Posting detailed information about your company and open vacancies on the official news page of our website \[(link)].

2\. **Offline presentation:** Your offers will be presented as a separate slide at the main event - Open Days.

### Key dates:

\- 03/21/2026 - Presentation of vacancies at the offline event (Open Days).

\- 03/23/2026 - Official start of career week and publication of materials on the website.

Karazin Career Week is a great opportunity to meet new people, expand your career prospects, and find new opportunities!
