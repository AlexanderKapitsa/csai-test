On 29 August 2024, a significant meeting was held between EPAM CAMPUS and the Educational and Research Institute of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (ESI) as part of a partnership agreement. EPAM was represented by Denys Hrynev, EPAM University Programme Manager in Ukraine, and Ivan Mikheiev, a leading specialist in the programme. The institute was represented by Director Dmytro Uzlov, Deputy Director Serhii Yakovliev, department heads, academic staff, postgraduate students and undergraduates.

<NewsPicture src="../../assets/photo_2024-10-24_21-23-54 (3).jpg" alt="Representatives of ESI and AI. Photographer: Sofia Dolinina (inst: dolininasofiiaal)" />

During the meeting, EPAM representatives presented the EPAM LEARNING educational platform, to which teachers and students received free access. The possibilities of integrating the platform's materials into the institute's academic disciplines and educational programs, as well as the prospects for improving the ESI educational and professional programs themselves, were discussed. The meeting laid the foundation for further development of cooperation, which is actively continuing.
