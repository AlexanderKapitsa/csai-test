We warmly congratulate Ivan Byzov, a PhD student of the Department of Mathematical Modeling and Data Analysis, and his academic supervisor, Professor of the Department, Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, Professor Serhii Yakovlev, on the successful defense of the dissertation in Specialty 122 Computer Science entitled "Information Technology for Automating Server Infrastructure Management Using DevOps Tools." The dissertation was successfully defended on July 23, 2026, at a meeting of the Ad Hoc Specialized Academic Council of V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University.

We wish Ivan continued inspiration, professional growth, and many future scientific achievements!
