We sincerely congratulate Maryna Yesina, Head of the Department, Candidate of Technical Sciences, Associate Professor, on her successful professional development training!

From March 2 to April 10, 2026, she completed the дистанційний express course “Fundamentals of Testology and Test Item Development” (30 hours / 1 ECTS credit).

During the course, she mastered:

* the modern classification of test items and basic terminology;
* the structure of test items and the requirements for their components;
* the impact of testing experience on individual performance and methods for increasing validity;
* Bloom’s taxonomy of educational objectives and the procedure for expert evaluation of test items.

The acquired knowledge will make it possible to develop test items of even higher quality, particularly for the Unified Professional Entrance Examination.

We are proud of the professional growth of our lecturers and wish her continued success and new achievements!
