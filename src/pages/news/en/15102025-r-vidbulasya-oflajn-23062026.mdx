On 15 October 2025, an offline meeting took place between representatives of the Educational and Scientific Institute of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (ESI CSAI) of V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University and students of Gymnasium № 148 and Lyceums № 150 and № 154 in the safe educational space of the Kharkiv Metro (Peremoha station).

The students were addressed by the Director of the ESI CSAI, Associate Professor D. Yu. Uzlov, the Head of the Department, Associate Professor M. V. Yesina, Senior Lecturer V. S. Korshenko, and Associate Professor S. V. Diadiun. They provided information about V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University, the Educational and Scientific Institute of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, admission and study at the Institute, the academic specialities offered, the Institute’s departments, and employment opportunities after graduation. They also answered students’ questions about artificial intelligence, robotics, cybersecurity, programming languages, and other topics.
