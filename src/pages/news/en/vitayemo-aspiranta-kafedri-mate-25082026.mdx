We congratulate Yegor Havryliuk, a postgraduate student of the Department of Mathematical Modeling and Data Analysis, and his academic supervisor, Professor Serhii Vsevolodovych Yakovlev, Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences, Professor of the Department, on the successful defense of the dissertation for the specialty 122 “Computer Science” on the topic “Models, Methods, and Information Technology of Hybrid Evolutionary-Swarm Optimization in Coverage Problems in Geographically Distributed Systems.”

The defense took place at a meeting of the One-Time Specialized Academic Council of V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University on August 21, 2026.

We wish Yegor and his supervisor inspiration and continued creative success!
