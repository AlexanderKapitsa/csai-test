Dear Colleagues!

We invite you to participate in the 12th International Scientific and Technical Conference “Intelligent Technologies in Interdisciplinary Research (ITIR-2026),” which will take place on November 16–18, 2026, at V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University.
The conference will be held in a hybrid format, allowing for both in-person participation and remote attendance via audio and video conferencing.\
We invite researchers, faculty members, graduate students, and undergraduates involved in research to participate.

Please submit your application to participate in the conference and your abstract to the following email address: [kmht@karazin.ua](mailto\:kmht@karazin.ua)
