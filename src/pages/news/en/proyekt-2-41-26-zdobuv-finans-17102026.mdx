Wonderful and inspiring news for our academic community! 🚀

Research Project, titled "Methods and tools for training and evaluating large language models for multimodal data analysis of low-resource languages," has emerged victorious in the competitive selection of research projects organized by the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine!

This recognition highlights the crucial importance of our research for the future of Ukrainian artificial intelligence. In an era where AI is advancing exponentially, ensuring the full-fledged development, robust evaluation, and dedicated support of the Ukrainian language within large multimodal models is a matter of our national scientific and technological sovereignty.

We extend our warmest congratulations to the Principal Investigator — Oleksii Turuta, PhD, Associate Professor ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-0970-8617)) — and the entire research team on this significant and well-deserved success!

We take immense pride in our researchers! We wish the team endless inspiration, productive work, breakthrough achievements, and new milestones on the global scientific stage! 👏✨
