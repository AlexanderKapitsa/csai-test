On 12 November 2025, an offline meeting took place between representatives of the Educational and Scientific Institute of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence (ESI CSAI) of V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University and graduating students together with 10th-grade students of Kharkiv Lyceum № 169 in the Institute’s safe educational space.

The meeting was attended by 35 students of Kharkiv Lyceum No. 169 and the school administration. The students were addressed by Senior Lecturer V. S. Korshenko, Senior Lecturer V. V. Vilihura, Associate Professor S. V. Diadiun, student I. Malko, and Senior Lecturer V. V. Kopieikin, who provided information about admission to and study at the Institute, the academic specialities offered, the Institute’s departments, and employment opportunities available after graduation. They also answered the students’ questions.

The head of the student computer club, student Ivan Malko, spoke about the exciting projects that students are developing within the club and invited the school students to join its activities. Senior Lecturer V. S. Korshenko demonstrated interesting developments in the field of artificial intelligence, while Senior Lecturer V. V. Kopieikin presented the Institute’s latest developments in robotics.
