### Education & Academic Degrees

- **1976:** Graduated with Honors in Research Engineering from Kharkiv
  Polytechnic Institute ("KhPI").
- **1978–1981:** Ph.D. studies at Technische Universität Dresden (Germany).
  Earned Doctor of Engineering (**Dr.-Ing.**) degree in Software Engineering in
  October 1981.
- **2000–2003:** Postdoctoral research studies at NTU "KhPI" and long-term
  research fellowship at the Institute of Informatics, University of Klagenfurt
  (Austria).
- **2006:** Earned **Doctor of Sciences (D.Sc.) in Engineering** degree
  (Specialty 05.13.06 — Information Technology) at NTU "KhPI".
- **2008:** Awarded the academic title of **Full Professor** by the Ministry of
  Education and Science of Ukraine.

---

### Professional Experience

- **2008–2018:** Professor, Department of Software Engineering and Management
  Intelligent Technologies, NTU "KhPI".
- **2018–2023:** Head of the Department of System Modeling and Technologies,
  Faculty of Computer Sciences, V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University.
- **2023–2024:** Acting Head of the Department of System Modeling and
  Technologies, Faculty of Computer Sciences, V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National
  University.
- **2024–Present:** Professor, Department of Intelligent Software Systems and
  Technologies, Educational and Scientific Institute of Computer Science and
  Artificial Intelligence, V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University.
