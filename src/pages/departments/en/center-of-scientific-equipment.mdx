about: >-
  The Center for Shared Use of Scientific Equipment “Laboratory of Computer
  Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, and Robotics” was established
  at the Educational and Research Institute of Computer Science and Artificial
  Intelligence at V. N. Karazin Kharkiv National University
longAbout: >
  The purpose of establishing the “Laboratory of Computer Science, Artificial
  Intelligence, Cybersecurity, and Robotics” Center for Shared Use of Scientific
  Equipment is to provide access to scientific equipment for research and
  development by domestic and foreign scientists in accordance with the
  conditions set forth in the regulations governing access to and use of
  scientific equipment.


  The Center provides its services in accordance with approved work plans,
  requests from the University’s departments, and orders from external
  individuals and legal entities.
keyAreas:
  - title: >
      **Academic** — supporting the educational process; organizing and
      conducting classes, experiments, and research with higher education
      students to help them gain practical mastery of specific theoretical
      concepts in academic disciplines
  - title: >
      **Scientific** — scientific and scientific-technical activities aimed at
      acquiring and applying new scientific knowledge in the course of
      conducting fundamental, applied, and exploratory research; the practical
      implementation of scientific findings in the educational process,
      manufacturing, and the service sector; ensuring high-quality training of
      specialists, as well as highly qualified scientific and
      scientific-pedagogical personnel; and working with grant programs
  - title: >
      **Innovation** — creating proprietary IT products; supporting, developing,
      and commercializing new types of technology, materials, and the like to
      ensure the innovative development of society and the national economy;
      solving complex problems in the field of scientific and high-tech
      development; enhancing the nation’s defense capabilities and security; and
      managing grant programs
